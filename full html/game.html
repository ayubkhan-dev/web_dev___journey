How to play

Use ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D to move the turtle.

Collect stars to score points. Avoid crabs (enemies).

Collecting a star spawns a new one. Survive as long as you can!

Press Space to pause/resume during play.
Tips: Move smoothly, don't touch crabs. Update difficulty = more crabs faster.
